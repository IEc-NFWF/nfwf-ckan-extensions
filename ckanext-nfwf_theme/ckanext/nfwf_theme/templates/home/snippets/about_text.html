The NFWF Coastal Resilience Open Data Platform provides access to the monitoring data collected or generated by grantees as part of NFWF-funded coastal resiliency programs. This searchable data platform is designed to store each grantee monitoring project’s metadata and data files for discovery and sharing among the grantees and wider community. Datasets include both spatial and tabular data and span ecological and socioeconomic topics for coastal restoration activities at locations funded by each program. A list of the NFWF programs currently participating in this platform is available on the Programs page.

This data platform is part of a broader effort to provide a transparent means of reporting the measurable impacts of NFWF-funded coastal resiliency projects. A related metrics database and reporting tool is being developed that will be used by NFWF to assess the impacts and cost-effectiveness of funding different types of resiliency projects. Funding for development of these systems is provided under the Hurricane Sandy Coastal Resiliency Competitive Grant Program and is supported by Abt Associates.
