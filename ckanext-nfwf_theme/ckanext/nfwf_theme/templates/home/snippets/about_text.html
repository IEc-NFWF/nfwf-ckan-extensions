The Coastal Resilience Open Data Platform, hosted by NFWF, provides access to monitoring data collected or generated by grantees and partners through NFWF programs that fund conservation for coastal resilience. This searchable data platform is designed to store metadata and data files for discovery and sharing among NFWF grantees, partners, and the wider conservation community. Datasets include both spatial and tabular data and span ecological and socioeconomic topics for coastal restoration activities at locations funded by each program. A list of the NFWF programs currently participating in this platform is available on the Programs page.

This data platform is part of a broader effort to provide a transparent means of reporting the measurable impacts of coastal resilience projects. A related metrics database and reporting tool, the Coastal Resilience Dashboard, tracks and communicates the impacts and cost-effectiveness of funding different types of coastal resilience projects.

Grantee Resources

NFWF has developed a number of resources to assist grantees with meeting their monitoring data collection and sharing requirements, such as monitoring protocols, data templates, the Open Data Platform User Manual, and responses to frequently asked questions. These resources are available at the NFWF NCRF Grantee Resources page.
